Dérive: AR-Dwaaltocht

Dérive is a walkable, immersive audio drama without a screen. Wearing headphones and a tracker, you move through a space where spatial sound and theatrical lighting respond to you. Your route determines which scenes you activate: as you wander, sound, light, and story shift around you.

In Dérive, you follow a TV salesperson and a journalist as they encounter the “spectacle”: a world where images come between us and shape how we see one another. As you wander, you activate scenes that bring this to life, from busy streets with flashing lights and passing ambulances to an invitation to a hidden jazz bar. Each step reveals another layer of the story. How can our perception change when the image is no longer in charge?

Artistic Research

VR & Theater

2022 – 2023 · Cross-tic · VR & Theatre

In this artistic research project, we (Stijn Bolhuis and Alexander) explore the dynamic convergence of theater and virtual reality (VR). Through a series of insightful interviews conducted in VRChat, we investigate how theater elements are translated and reimagined in virtual environments. Uncover the fascinating ways in which VR platforms like VRChat facilitate the breaking of conventions, the emergence of new norms, and the unique interactions within this immersive space.
